# Competitor Analysis: Minimalist vs The Derma Co vs Plum

A digital marketing competitor analysis of three Indian D2C skincare brands that sell to the same young, ingredient-aware buyer. Each brand is compared on the same metrics across website, SEO, social media, content, and marketing.

**Objective:** learn how marketers evaluate competitors and identify opportunities.

## Repository contents

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `data/competitor-comparison.csv` | Competitor comparison sheet (all raw metrics) |
| `report/Competitor-Analysis-Report.pdf` | Full report: strengths and weaknesses, content and social comparison, opportunities |

## Tools used

Google Search, Similarweb, Ubersuggest, Instagram, YouTube, Meta Ad Library.

## Key findings

| Metric | Minimalist | The Derma Co | Plum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly visits | 3.49M | **4.85M** | 2.46M |
| Avg. visit duration | **2m 43s** | 50s | 1m 46s |
| Domain authority | 52 | 45 | **55** |
| Est. organic traffic | 355.4K | **818.8K** | 788.9K |
| Instagram followers | 526K | 659K | **1.1M** |
| Instagram engagement rate | **0.21%** | 0.08% | 0.03% |
| YouTube subscribers | 1.33K | 18.2K | **134K** |

- **Minimalist** has the most engaged audience (longest visits, highest Instagram engagement) but the weakest discovery: its top keywords are its own brand name.
- **The Derma Co** captures the most search demand, getting the most organic traffic from the fewest keywords through a daily "best X" blog.
- **Plum** has the biggest footprint (highest domain authority, most backlinks, largest Instagram and YouTube audiences) but the lowest engagement per post.

## Three marketing opportunities

1. **Capture non-brand search with concern-based guides.** Minimalist gets ~355K organic visits from 39.8K keywords vs The Derma Co's ~819K from 22.6K. Ingredient and skin-concern guides would reach shoppers who don't know the brand yet.
2. **Own ingredient education on YouTube.** Minimalist is the most education-led brand but has only 1.33K subscribers; Plum's 134K proves the audience exists.
3. **Turn questions into community content.** All three brands sit below 0.25% Instagram engagement, and Plum posts most but engages least. Live Q&As, comment-reply Reels, and customer routine stories target engagement rather than volume.

## Notes

Similarweb and Ubersuggest figures are third-party estimates, so differences between brands matter more than exact numbers. Engagement rate = (avg. likes + avg. comments) / followers x 100, based on each brand's last 10 non-pinned posts.
